# SAS Technology

We're the technology department at **Singapore American School**,
building and maintaining the internal tools, dashboards, and
integrations SAS runs on.

Most of what's here is private — it supports our own school
operations rather than the public. A few repos are public where that
makes sense (shared knowledge, tooling with no sensitive data).

- Org-wide contribution, security, and support policies: this
  repository (`.github`) — see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](https://github.com/sas-technology/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
  and [`SECURITY.md`](https://github.com/sas-technology/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
- Questions? Reach the SAS Technology team directly, or see
  [`SUPPORT.md`](https://github.com/sas-technology/.github/blob/main/SUPPORT.md).
