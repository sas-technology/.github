# Code of Conduct

SAS Technology repositories support the work of Singapore American
School. Everyone contributing here — staff, student interns, and any
outside collaborator — is expected to keep interactions professional,
respectful, and focused on the work.

## Our standard

- Be respectful in code review, issues, and discussion. Critique the
  code, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask questions before assuming a mistake was
  careless.
- No harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks, in any
  repository, issue, PR, or commit message.
- Treat student, staff, and family data with the care required by SAS
  policy and applicable law — see [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) for
  how to report a data-handling or security concern.

## Scope

This applies to every sas-technology repository and any space tied to
them (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages).

## Reporting

If something crosses the line, report it to the SAS Technology
department lead or to **[it-security@sas.edu.sg](mailto:it-security@sas.edu.sg)**.
Reports are handled privately and taken seriously.
